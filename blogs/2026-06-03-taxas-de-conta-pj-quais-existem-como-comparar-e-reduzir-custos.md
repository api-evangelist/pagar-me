This guide examines fees associated with PJ (business) accounts in Brazil, comparing different cost structures and offering strategies to minimize expenses. The article explains various tariffs including monthly maintenance, Pix, TED transfers, and boleto payments while emphasizing that true account costs emerge through actual business operations rather than advertised base rates.
