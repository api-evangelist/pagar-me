Criminals pose as customers to swap legitimate card readers with fraudulent devices during busy periods, redirecting sales to their account. The guide provides practical protection strategies including personalizing equipment with visible markings, verifying transaction receipts for correct business information, and maintaining constant supervision of payment devices. It also outlines steps to take if victimized by this scam.
